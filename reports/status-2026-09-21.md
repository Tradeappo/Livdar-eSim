## Page plan reconciliation

The handoff declared 1,342 planned pages. Its page plan rows add up to 1,331
and its cluster table to 1,334. None of those three numbers comes from a
committed script. The gap of 11 is one page per market for the 11 markets the
handoff counted, the size of its Homepage row.

The committed script (scripts/plan-report.mjs) gives 1,285 pages over 12
research markets. It counts en-us and en-gb separately, while the site has one
English URL per destination or topic. Converted to distinct site URLs, the plan
is 1,226 pages:

| State | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| live | 99 |
| eligible now (live locale, measured demand, facts in the dataset) | 265 |
| blocked, market not opened | 711 |
| blocked, data we must not invent (comparisons, compare hub) | 151 |
| rejected by rule (P4, zero demand) | not counted as pages |

The 300,000 figure is a scale target in data/programmatic-taxonomy.json, not a
plan. With the entity lists the repo holds (126 countries, 11 regions, no city,
district or airport list), the taxonomy reaches 97,818 combinations. The funnel
narrows the connectivity family to 173 destination combinations in live
locales. With this lot, 22 of them pass every hard gate on demand measured in
their own locale. Another 10 (9 of the 10 Romanian destination pages, all but Turkey,
and EN United Kingdom) pass every gate except local
demand and are published only by the plan's P1/P2 rule; their measured local
volume is reported as measured, down to zero, never rounded up to the floor.
Counts in the table above are with this lot applied.
