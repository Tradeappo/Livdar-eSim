Brazil is new and it changes the Portuguese decision. 60,390 addressable across 547 keywords, with a South American destination set (Argentina, Chile, Uruguay, Peru) that appears nowhere else in the dataset and commercial CPCs of 0.90 to 1.50 dollars. One warning that decides whether the market works at all: the Brazilian word is chip, not eSIM. "chip internacional" alone is 12,000 a month. A Brazilian page written in eSIM vocabulary will not rank. Portugal at 14,920 is now served from Brazilian Portuguese rather than given its own market.

The Arabic verdict changed, and not the way I expected. The first pass measured 310 a month and I was ready to reject the Gulf. Re-swept from the bare term, the region returns 32,590 a month of demand typed in Latin script English: esim dubai 4,400, esim uae 1,300, esim saudi arabia 1,400. Native Arabic phrasing exists but is only 5,100 and mostly domestic operator promotion. So the Gulf is real and worth having, and the right way to serve it is the English pages, not an Arabic market. Arabic as a publishing language stays rejected; the Gulf as an audience does not.

8. What this research still cannot tell you

  1. The last 16,720 to 2.5 million was left on the table deliberatelyReaching it meant spending the final 10,616 API units of the billing month on a 0.7 percent difference. The quota resets on 8 October and it is worth more then, checking whether the pages actually rank. The obvious places to spend it: Brazil has only had one pass, Japan and Taiwan were swept once each, and the European "esim" sweeps hit the row limit twice.
  2. The 825,000 baseline never reproducedAt the scope the original brief stated, Ahrefs returns 167,430. The baseline was most likely built from a much wider per city or per operator list, or a broader match method. It is a planning number that was never measured, and nothing here was bent to meet it.
  3. 902,540 of the raw total is brand navigation, and it is not oursPeople typing Airalo or Holafly. A comparison page catches a slice of it, and that slice is already counted inside the comparisons cluster rather than double counted here.
  4. 631,050 is bare category wordsThirty eight keywords: eSIM, roaming, MiFi and their equivalents. I moved bare roaming and MiFi into this tier during the audit of pass 4, which cost 56,600 off the addressable figure. Consistency was worth more than the number.
  5. Brazil will not rank on eSIM vocabularyThe word is chip. "chip internacional" is 12,000 a month on its own and carries a sixth of the market. A Brazilian page built from the English or German page's vocabulary will fail regardless of how well it is translated, which is the sharpest possible illustration of why these are separate markets.
  6. Part of the German and French sub-100 tail looks syntheticRoughly 25,000 to 30,000 a month clusters at 50 to 70 searches with template vocabulary, which reads like one competitor's programmatic page set rather than independent search behaviour. Real Ahrefs figures, thin signal.
  7. Indonesia, Turkey and Korea have the wrong searcherTheir travel relevant volume is largely foreigners searching in German, French, Polish and English about going there, not locals travelling out. Real inbound demand, served better from the English and European pages than from a native market.
  8. Pocket wifi straddles travel and domestic broadband"portable wifi" at 20,000 in the US and "wifi portatile" at 6,100 in Italy are kept because the brief named pocket and portable wifi explicitly. Bare "mifi" and "mifi device" were moved out during the audit as device purchase queries. The boundary is a judgement call and it is worth 38,450 either way.
  9. Missing keywords are not zeroesAhrefs omits a row when it has no tracked data. Romania had 29 of 53 terms missing in pass 1, and the Romanian complementary sweep returned nothing at all for every connectivity phrase tried. That is invisible demand, not absent demand.
  10. Difficulty is missing from passes 3 and 4It costs 10 units a row on top of the 13 unit base. Dropping it bought roughly 3,000 extra real keywords instead. It can be backfilled on a shortlist when the build reaches those pages.
  11. Mainland China is still unmeasurableAhrefs tracks no Baidu data. Taiwan and Hong Kong appear, the mainland never does. Any China decision needs a different source entirely.
