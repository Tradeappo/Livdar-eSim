## Template similarity

The score is the share of the rendered surface identical across sampled pages of the same family and locale, over simulated title, meta, H1 and uniqueness reason. A high score alone is not a defect, because a template is supposed to share its connecting words. It is a defect when the score is high AND the distinct data behind the pages is thin, so the two are always reported together.

No cell combines high similarity with thin distinct data at the thresholds above.
