## The pattern

Across every SERP sampled in this programme, in every market and every family, at
least one winner is under DR 20 with fewer than three referring domains. There is
no family here where links are the barrier. Recorded instances:

- Poland: DR 0 at position 8.
- Netherlands: DR 6 at position 10.
- Germany: zero-refdomain pages at positions 6 and 8 on a 257,000 volume term.
- Germany: kalender-online.com, DR 10, 105,485 visits.
- Krakow: DR 10 at position 7, DR 17 at position 8.
- Dublin: DR 0 with zero referring domains at position 6.

## What that means, and what it does not

It means a new site can rank here, which is the premise the whole programme rests
on and it is now evidenced in six independent places.

It does not mean pages win by existing. The low-DR winners all have something
specific: hellocracow.com has a fare and time comparison per mode, visitkrakow.com
has 11 referring domains and local depth, lisbonguru.com has a month-by-month
table for one city. The pages that lose on these SERPs are the ones with a
template and a number, and `weather-and-climate.com` is the cautionary case: DR 72,
programmatic at scale, and its Combleux and Mettray pages pull 34 and 22 visits
while ranking 8th and 10th for a query about Paris.

That is the shape of the risk this inventory is designed around. Scale without
per-entity substance produces DR 72 pages earning 22 visits.
