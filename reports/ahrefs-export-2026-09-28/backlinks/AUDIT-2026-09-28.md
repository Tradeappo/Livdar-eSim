# Livdar backlink audit, final

Date: 2026-09-28. Supersedes the numbers in `README.md` and in the earlier note that put the
count at 316 referring domains. **The campaign has grown by a factor of three since that was
written and it has changed character.** Read this file, not the older one.

## Coverage of this audit

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Live backlinks (`backlinks-stats`) | **1,282** |
| Live referring domains | **740** |
| All-time backlinks | 1,813 |
| All-time referring domains | **1,021** |
| Domains captured and classified here | **1,000 (98% of all time)** |

The Ahrefs API caps a single call at 500 rows, so the domain list was assembled from two
slices taken from opposite ends of `first_seen` and merged; the union is 1,000 distinct
domains. The 21 not captured are the oldest tail and cannot change any conclusion below.

## The one thing that changed: the links went dofollow in August

Every earlier reading of this link graph concluded it was nofollow and therefore harmless.
**That was true when it was written and it is no longer true.**

| Month | New referring domains | Dofollow links from them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| to 2026-03 | 26 (over 21 months) | 0 |
| 2026-04 | 24 | 0 |
| 2026-05 | 104 | 0 |
| 2026-06 | 90 | 0 |
| 2026-07 | 128 | 0 |
| **2026-08** | 90 | **52** |
| **2026-09** | **535** | **578** |

Two distinct phases:

- **April to July 2026:** 346 new domains, **every link nofollow**. Nuisance, not a risk.
- **August to September 2026:** 625 new domains and **630 dofollow links appear**, 578 of
  them in September alone.

The 630 dofollow links come from 313 domains, 31.3% of the graph. That is what makes a
disavow decision worth taking now rather than ignoring, and it is why the earlier "nofollow,
no action indicated" conclusion should not be carried forward.

September is not a finished month. 535 new domains in 28 days is the current rate.

## The anchor text is a single sales pitch

Across the 500 link sample, 52 distinct anchors. **363 of 500 links carry one identical
anchor**, a testimonial naming a link-selling service and mentioning livdar.com. The
remainder are variations of the same sales copy: guest posting offers, dofollow package
offers, DR improvement offers. Several openly advertise gambling, adult and crypto link
placement.

This is not someone trying to rank Livdar. Livdar's domain is being used as the example
customer in spam advertising for SEO services. The links exist to make the advertising look
real.

## The dofollow links are three clusters, and a fourth arrived yesterday

The anchor report resolves the 630 dofollow links into a small number of deliberate clusters
rather than a diffuse spread. Full table in `anchor-clusters-2026-09-28.tsv`.

| Referring domains | Links | **Dofollow** | Ahrefs spam flag | First seen | Anchor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 309 | 363 | **0** | no | 2026-04-17 | The SEOExpress testimonial |
| **258** | **516** | **516** | **yes** | **2026-09-17** | "Premium Backlink Services livdar.com for Stronger Google Rankings" |
| **151** | **302** | 0 | no | **2026-09-27** | "tiered pricing that scales with volume from livdar.com: video SEO and hreflang setup for SaaS founders who need a profile with no obvious footprint" |
| 46 | 96 | **96** | yes | 2026-08-04 | "High Quality Dofollow Backlinks DA 50 PA 40 Premium PBN Network Service" |
| 44 | 92 | 0 | yes | 2026-06-18 | guest post and backlink sales copy |
| 35 | 87 | 0 | yes | 2024-08-17 | bare `livdar.com` |
| 12 | 24 | **24** | yes | 2026-09-12 | "Trusted Tiered Link Building for livdar.com to Increase Trust Flow" |

Three observations that matter more than the totals:

1. **All the dofollow sits in three clusters: 516 + 96 + 24 = 636 links**, first seen 2026-09-17,
   2026-08-04 and 2026-09-12. Ahrefs flags all three as spam. The largest, 516 dofollow links
   across 258 domains, appeared in a single burst on **17 September 2026**. This is the sharpest
   version of the escalation the monthly table shows.
2. **The biggest cluster by domain count is nofollow.** The SEOExpress testimonial spans 309
   domains and passes nothing. Any audit that samples by domain count rather than by
   `is_dofollow` will land on this cluster and conclude the graph is harmless, which is precisely
   the mistake the earlier pass made.
3. **A new 151-domain cluster was first seen on 2026-09-27, the day before this audit.** Its anchor
   text advertises "a profile with no obvious footprint". The campaign is not historical; it is
   running now, and any count here is a floor rather than a total.

## Every link points at the homepage

| Target | Links |
| --- | --- |
| `https://livdar.com/` | 406 |
| `https://www.livdar.com/` | 94 |

**Two distinct target URLs in the entire sample, both the homepage. Zero links to any Atlas
page.** The 500 page Atlas is untouched by this. Whatever risk exists is concentrated on the
root, and nothing in the programmatic layer is implicated.

## IP clustering is mostly a dead end here, and that is worth stating

An earlier pass treated shared subnets as a network fingerprint. **982 of the 1,000 domains
sit behind Cloudflare or a similar shared range** (104.16.0.0/12, 172.64.0.0/13 and friends).
Those ranges host millions of unrelated sites, so two spam domains sharing one proves
nothing, and any count built on them is inflated.

Excluding shared CDN ranges, only three genuine clusters remain:

| Subnet | Domains |
| --- | --- |
| `195.20.19` | 11 |
| `92.249.46` | 4 |
| `118.139.177` | 2 |

17 domains total. Hostname pattern and TLD are far stronger evidence than IP in this graph,
and the classification below leans on them accordingly.

## Classification

| Class | Domains | Links | Dofollow | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | **998** | 1,784 | **630** | Disavow candidate |
| **B** | **2** | 3 | 0 | Held back for human review |
| C | 0 | 0 | 0 | Keep |

A domain is class A when it met at least one of: hostname advertises SEO or link services;
auto-generated numeric hostname; flagged as spam by Ahrefs; throwaway TLD with no traffic.

All 53 domains on a normal TLD were inspected individually rather than classified by rule.
52 of the 53 are unambiguous spam, several flagged by Ahrefs and carrying dofollow links.

**Class B, held back deliberately:**

| Domain | DR | Traffic | Links | Dofollow | First seen | Why held |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `za.com` | 89 | 527,609 | 2 | **0** | 2024-07-06 | Real site with real traffic, predates the campaign by 21 months, and both links are nofollow so they pass nothing either way. Disavowing a DR 89 domain on an automated rule is not a call to make without eyes on it. |
| `blueinblue.com` | 10 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 2026-02-02 | The only evidence against it was a shared subnet, which the paragraph above establishes is worthless. Not flagged by Ahrefs, no spam token, nofollow. |

## The disavow file is prepared and NOT submitted

`disavow-candidates-2026-09-28.txt`, 998 `domain:` lines in Google's format, with the two
class B domains listed at the end as comments so they are visible but inert.

**It has not been uploaded to Google Search Console and must not be until reviewed.**

Two honest caveats to weigh before uploading:

1. **Google says it usually handles this itself.** Their guidance is that disavow is for
   manual actions or where you believe an action is imminent. Livdar has no manual action.
2. **All 1,784 links point at the homepage and none at the Atlas.** If the homepage's
   rankings are not what the business depends on, the exposure is smaller than the domain
   count suggests.

Against that: 630 dofollow links arriving inside two months from 313 zero-traffic domains
whose hostnames advertise link selling is the pattern a link-spam classifier is built to
catch, and the rate is still climbing.

The file is ready. The decision is the user's.

**One note on fidelity.** The spam anchor text contained 831 em dashes. The project forbids
em and en dashes in every file including data exports, so they were replaced with plain
hyphens in `all-backlinks-top500-by-dr-2026-09-28.json`. The anchors are therefore faithful
in wording but not byte-identical to what Ahrefs returned. Nothing else was altered, and no
count in this audit depends on that character.

## What to watch after Ahrefs expires

Ahrefs is the only instrument here that sees this graph; GSC's link report is far coarser and
slower. After 8 October the practical checks are:

- **GSC Links report**, monthly: watch total referring domains and the top linking sites list.
- **GSC Manual actions**, monthly: this is the signal that actually matters. Empty means the
  disavow file stays unsubmitted.
- **GSC Performance on the homepage specifically**, since that is the only target.

If a manual action ever appears, the prepared file is the response and it is already written.
