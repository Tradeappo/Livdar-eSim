# Link intersect: a negative result worth recording

The question was which domains link to Livdar's competitors and not to Livdar,
as outreach targets. The answer is that there is very little here, and the
reason is more useful than a list would have been.

## What absentify's real links are

Filtered to `is_spam: false` and source traffic above 1,000, ordered by source
traffic, top 30:

**SaaS directories and software listing sites**, which is most of them:
`g2.com` (DR 91), `saashub.com` (DR 80, 106 links), `softwareworld.co` (DR 73,
42 links), `techimply.com` (DR 73), `betalist.com` (DR 77),
`appsruntheworld.com` (DR 69), `saasadviser.co` (DR 70), `subscribed.fyi`,
`apis.io`, `webcatalog.io` (150 links), `logiciels.pro`.

**microsoft.com, DR 96, 47 dofollow links**, first seen 2023. absentify is a
Microsoft Teams application, which is where that comes from.

**German regional news**: `hasepost.de` (DR 65, **136 dofollow**),
`ulm-news.de` (DR 51, 7 dofollow), `wochenblatt-news.de`, `hallelife.de`,
`itwelt.at`, `wirtschaftsforum.de`.

A few product adjacents: `buddypunch.com`, `vacationtracker.io`, `qovery.com`,
`wavebox.io`.

## Why almost none of it transfers

absentify's links come from **being a SaaS product**, not from being a holiday
content site. Livdar is not a Teams app, is not in a software category, and
cannot be listed in a SaaS directory on the same basis. The single largest
source, Microsoft, is a platform relationship.

And the decisive point: **absentify's holiday pages rank with one backlink
each.** Its NRW page takes position 6 and 6,151 monthly visits on 1 backlink
from 1 referring domain. Its French page is position 6 with the same. The links
above are not what makes those pages rank, so acquiring them would not make
Livdar's equivalent pages rank either.

## What is actually transferable

The German regional news sites, which publish press release style material:
`hasepost.de`, `ulm-news.de`, `wochenblatt-news.de`, `hallelife.de`. That is a
small, low-authority set, and it is the kind of link that arrives by paying for
distribution rather than by earning attention.

## So: not worth pursuing now

For the surfaces Livdar is strongest on, links are not the constraint. Every
holiday SERP read in this snapshot has pages at positions 4 to 8 with 0 or 1
backlinks. Spending effort on outreach to compete there would be solving a
problem that does not exist.

Where links would matter is the hard end: `kalkulator wynagrodzeń` at KD 43
against `zarobki.pracuj.pl` with 1,800 referring domains, `salary calculator`
at KD 69, `cost of living comparison` at KD 81. Those are not near term
targets, so the links are not near term either.

Recorded so the question does not get asked again without this in front of it.

## Broken backlinks

Not pursued, for the same reason. Broken backlink reclamation trades effort for
links, and links are not the binding constraint on the surfaces worth building.
Worth revisiting if and when Livdar competes for the hard calculator terms.
