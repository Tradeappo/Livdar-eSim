# Link opportunities, taken from a competitor's real profile

Date: 2026-09-29. **FROZEN_AFTER_AHREFS_EXPIRY.**

## Method, and why it is this rather than a wishlist

The brief asks for legitimate backlink opportunities: tourism boards, universities, government,
local portals, tool directories, niche communities. A list like that is easy to invent and useless
to act on.

So instead of guessing, this reads the referring domains of **`feiertage-deutschland.de`**, which
is the most link-efficient competitor found anywhere in this research: DR 36, 167,105 monthly
visits from 116 pages, **1,441 traffic per page against absentify's 128**. It is close enough to
Livdar's position to be a realistic template, and far enough ahead to have earned links worth
copying.

Every row is a domain that actually links to that competitor, with its DR, traffic, link count,
dofollow count and first-seen date. `prospects-from-competitor-profiles-2026-09-29.tsv`, 40 rows.

## What the profile actually shows

**Government and city portals are the strongest pattern, and they pass links.**

| Domain | DR | Dofollow links | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `hamburg.de` | 89 | **2,917** | One city portal, 2,917 dofollow links |
| `berlin.de` | 91 | 3 | |
| `sachsen.de` | 90 | 1 | |
| `poznan.pl` | 86 | 1 | Polish, which is where Livdar is weakest |

The Hamburg number is the standout finding in this file. A city portal that decides your holiday
calendar is the reference it wants to cite will link to it at scale, and those links are dofollow.

**Universities cite term dates and holiday calendars, and they pass links.**

`uni-bremen.de` (DR 83, 12 dofollow), `uni-bonn.de` (84, 2), `uni-freiburg.de` (84, 1),
`uni-marburg.de` (81, 2). Also `ac-versailles.fr` (DR 78), a French education authority, which is
nofollow but exactly relevant to the French school-holiday family.

**Niche peers cross-link heavily.** `feiertage-oesterreich.at` sends **4,225 dofollow** links and
`calovo.de` sends 362. Recorded as MEDIUM rather than HIGH because 4,225 sitewide links almost
certainly means a sister site rather than an earned link, and copying that pattern would mean
owning both sites.

**A portal cluster worth understanding before imitating.** `web.de`, `gmx.net`, `gmx.at`,
`gmx.ch`, `1und1.de` and `aol.de` all appear, all dofollow, and the first five were all first seen
within six days of each other in late August and early September 2026. That is not six editorial
decisions; it is one syndication or widget arrangement. Tagged INVESTIGATE, because the mechanism
matters more than the links.

## What to exclude, and why it is listed rather than silently dropped

11 of the 40 rows are marked EXCLUDE:

- **UGC platforms:** `github.com`, `blogspot.com`, `t.me`, `telegram.me`, `neocities.org`. Anyone
  can create these, so they are not a signal.
- **Metrics scrapers:** `sitelike.org` (465 links), `siteprice.org` (310), `similarsites.com` (12).
  Auto-generated, all nofollow, zero value, and they will appear for any domain that exists.
- **Flagged spam:** `za.com`, `pages.dev`, `c99.nl`, `socastsrm.com`. Note `za.com` is the same
  domain sitting in Livdar's own class B review list, which is a useful cross-check: it links to
  competitors too, so it is a general spammer rather than anything targeted at Livdar.

## The honest limits of this file

**One competitor, one market.** This is `feiertage-deutschland.de` in Germany. The equivalent pull
for `kalendarzswiat.pl`, `kalender-365.nl` and `vacances-scolaires-education.fr` would cost about
960 units each and would probably show the same shape: government, universities, niche peers,
regional press. It was not run, and that is a gap rather than a conclusion.

**No outreach list, no contact details, no templates.** This is a taxonomy of what kinds of sites
link to this content and how, not a campaign.

**Nothing here was acted on.** No outreach was sent and none should be inferred as approved.

## What survives Ahrefs expiring

The taxonomy does. After 8 October the categories still tell you where to look:

1. **City and state portals** in the markets Livdar publishes for. The Hamburg pattern says a city
   portal will link at scale if your calendar is the one it wants to cite.
2. **Universities**, which publish term dates and need a holiday reference.
3. **Regional press**, which writes the same holiday story every year and needs a source.
4. **Housing and relocation companies** (`leg-wohnen.de`) and **logistics companies**
   (`eurosender.com`), which map onto the rent and moving-cost surfaces directly.

What will not survive is the ability to check who links to a competitor today. Capture any further
competitor profiles before 8 October or lose that instrument entirely; GSC shows only Livdar's own
links, never anyone else's.
