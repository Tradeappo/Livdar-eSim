# absentify.com: the programmatic model Livdar is already building

Found by SERP analysis, not by guessing. absentify appears in the German
holiday SERP at position 6 and the French one at position 6, on the same
keywords Livdar's Pulse pages target.

It is worth studying closely because **its URL structure is almost identical to
Livdar's** and its results show what the family is worth when it works.

Note what that table is: **one page earning 6,151 monthly visits from position

to win the SERP to be worth building, because it collects hundreds of long tail
variants around the head term.

It also ranks with genuinely broken URLs: `/Public` earns 3,829 visits and
`/Home/Días%20festivos/espana` earns 778. Livdar's URLs are better than that
already.

## The replicable pattern

1. One page per subdivision, not one page per country. The subdivision pages
   out-earn the country pages (NRW 6,151 against Germany 4,159).
2. Accept positions 5 to 9. The long tail around the head term is where the
   traffic is.
3. Publish the same family in every market that has subdivisions and a
   language you already serve. absentify covers DE, AT, CH, AU, CA, US, FR, ES.
4. It needs no links. absentify's NRW page ranks at position 6 with **1
   backlink from 1 referring domain**.

Livdar already does 1 and has the machinery for 3. What it is missing is
coverage: five German Länder, and the entire en-AU, en-CA and fr-CA
opportunity.
