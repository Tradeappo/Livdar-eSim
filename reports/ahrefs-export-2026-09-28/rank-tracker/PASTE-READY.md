## Read this first: why two phases and not 82 batches

Every keyword here carries a unique tag combination (surface, family, language, market,
cohort), so tagging at paste time would mean **82 separate paste operations**. That is not a
reasonable thing to ask of anyone.

Country, however, genuinely must be set per batch, and there are only nine. So:

- **Phase 1**, nine pastes: get all 125 keywords in, tagged only with `atlas`.
- **Phase 2**, at your own pace: bulk-tag using the lists further down. Select a group in the
  keyword table, apply one tag, move on.

**Phase 1 is the part with the deadline.** Once the keywords exist, Rank Tracker starts
collecting position history, and that history is the thing that cannot be backfilled after
8 October. Tags are organisational and can be added in November if need be.
