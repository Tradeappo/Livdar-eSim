# New families found, and the two that survived a SERP check

Discovery, not selection. Nothing here is built and no cohort is chosen.

The method matters, because search difficulty misled this analysis once
already. Every candidate family below was measured for demand and then
**checked against its actual SERP**, and two of the four did not survive that.

**Cost of living in Taiwanese Chinese is effectively zero.** Livdar's largest

weekend derivative, not the cost of living pages that would have been the
obvious port.

## 4. Year calendar head terms: DOES NOT SURVIVE

This looked like the biggest find of the day and it is not a Livdar
opportunity.

| Market | Keyword | Volume | KD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FR | calendrier 2026 | **901,000** | 51 |
| US | 2026 calendar | **433,000** | 39 |
| PL | kalendarz 2026 | **430,000** | **2** |
| NL | kalender 2026 | **224,000** | **0** |
| US | federal holidays 2026 | 158,000 | 47 |
| US | us holidays 2026 | 34,000 | 62 |
| PL | dni wolne 2026 | 15,000 | **0** |
| NL | vrije dagen 2026 | 15,000 | 2 |

430,000 searches at KD 2 and 224,000 at KD 0 are not credible, and the SERP
says why.

**SERP check, `kalendarz 2026`:** position 1 `kalendarzswiat.pl`, DR 47 but
**URL Rating 29 with 127 backlinks and 245,119 monthly visits on that single
URL**. Position 2 `kalbi.pl`, UR 32. Position 3 `kalendarz-365.pl`, **55,463
backlinks from 455 referring domains**. Position 5 `bimkal.pl`, 6,964
backlinks. Then Canva at DR 93 and Google Play at DR 100.

These are dedicated calendar businesses with real page level authority, which
is exactly what Livdar's pages do not have. The KD figure is wrong here in the
way KD 3 was wrong for `best time to visit japan`.

`dni wolne 2026` at 15,000 KD 0 and `vrije dagen 2026` at 15,000 KD 2 are a
different matter: those are holiday queries without a year-calendar intent and
they belong with family 1, not here.

## What this adds up to

Every family that survived is Pulse or a derivative of Pulse. That is now the
third independent line of evidence pointing the same way, after demand
(1,203,210 searches, 51 per cent of the total on 11 per cent of the pages) and
SERP weakness.

And one thing worth stating plainly, because it is the opposite of what page
counts suggest: **Move is 193 of the 500 pages, 2.8 per cent of the demand,
and has no market in Taiwanese Chinese at all.** No family found today points
at extending it.
