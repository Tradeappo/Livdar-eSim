Four keywords, 772,000 monthly searches, held by pages with no links. The matrix is 16 states
x 6 holiday periods x year.

**It is blocked, and the blocker is not SEO.** School holiday dates are set and published by each
of the 16 state education ministries. Building this requires a licensed or openly published
dataset, and the project forbids scraping. Until someone confirms a lawful source with adequate
coverage and update cadence, **the 772,000 is a measurement, not a plan.** It is recorded at the
top of the inventory so it is not lost, and marked BLOCKED so it is not mistaken for ready work.

This is the single highest-value open question from the whole Ahrefs exercise.

## The risk that applies to the whole inventory

**Almost every German and Australian term checked carries `ai_overview` in its SERP features.**
`fronleichnam feiertag wo`, `feiertage hamburg 2026`, `feiertage bremen 2026`,
`feiertage schleswig holstein 2026`, `feiertage rheinland pfalz 2026`, `nsw public holidays 2026`,
`queensland public holidays 2026`, `sa public holidays 2026`, `act public holidays 2026`,
`tasmania public holidays 2026`, `public holidays 2026`: all of them.

A holiday date is one fact. It is the ideal AI Overview answer and the worst kind of query to
depend on for clicks. Two things follow:

1. **The Canadian terms are the exception.** None of the Canadian holiday SERPs checked showed an
   AI Overview. That is a point in Canada's favour that pure volume does not show.
2. **The defensible intent is the part an Overview cannot finish:** bridge days, long weekends,
   how to combine leave to get nine days off, which needs several dates and a calculation rather
   than one date. `long weekend 2026` in Canada is 18,000 searches and their page holds position 5
   with zero referring domains.

`scalability_pages`, `livdar_coverage_now`, `gsc_evidence`, `notes`.

`gsc_evidence` is `out_of_window` for every row, and that is the accurate value rather than a
convenient one: no Atlas page has a measurable Google reading yet, for the reasons in
`../gsc/GSC-X-AHREFS.md`. It is not zero and must not be recorded as zero.

`monetization_fit` is **medium at best across the entire inventory**, and that deserves saying
plainly. Only 16 of the 500 existing Atlas pages reach the eSIM shop, and zero do in seven of
nine languages. Someone looking up whether Corpus Christi is a holiday in Bavaria is not close to
buying a travel eSIM. Every candidate here is an audience play, not a revenue play, and the
revenue path is a separate unsolved problem.

## If only one thing gets built

The five German states and the named-holiday pivot. Together: **roughly 55,000 monthly searches,
KD 0 to 5, no new dataset, no new language, no new template.** Everything else needs either a
data licence or a new country dataset.

And per `../competitors/COMPETITOR-INTELLIGENCE.md`, publish nothing before the internal link
imbalance is understood: Pulse carries 51.2% of Livdar's demand on 9.7% of its internal links.
Adding pages to an under-linked surface compounds the problem rather than fixing it.
